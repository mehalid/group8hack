## Our Tool 💡🔋
- **Description:** The system analyzes input data defined by the team to perform statistical evaluations, including standard descriptive statistics, control chart generation for a ten-panel power output sample, power output frequency distribution plotting, and estimation of the mean time to failure along with its confidence interval.
- **User:** NYSERDA Consultant – A data-driven energy systems consultant working on behalf of the New York State Energy Research & Development Authority (NYSERDA). The user specializes in renewable energy performance assessment, reliability analysis, and statistical quality control for large-scale solar power infrastructure. Their goal is to evaluate panel health, identify early signs of degradation, and extend the operational lifespan of solar farms across New York State through data-informed maintenance and decision-making.
